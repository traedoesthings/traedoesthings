Cuban-style mojo pork made fast in the Instant Pot, with Coca-Cola in the marinade for a sticky, caramelized glaze. Pile it on charred corn tortillas with quick pickled red onions and a tart cilantro-lime crema. It was built to sit next to a creamy Sri Lankan shrimp curry at a dinner party, but it holds its own anywhere you need a crowd-friendly entree.

## At a Glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Yield | 6 to 8 servings (about 16 to 24 tacos) |
| Active time | 30 minutes |
| Total time | About 2 hours, plus marinating time if you have it |
| Equipment | 6-quart or larger Instant Pot, sheet pan, blender |
| Make ahead | Pork reheats well in its juices. Crema and onions keep for days. |

## Ingredients

### Pork

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Boneless pork shoulder, cut into 2 to 3 inch chunks | 3 to 4 lb |
| Kosher salt | 1 tbsp |
| Coca-Cola (regular, not diet) | 3/4 cup |
| Fresh lime juice | 1/4 cup |
| Orange juice | 1/4 cup |
| Garlic cloves, smashed | 8 to 10 |
| Ground cumin | 1 tbsp |
| Dried oregano | 2 tsp |
| Olive oil | 1/4 cup |
| Black pepper | 1 tsp |
| Yellow onion, sliced (optional) | 1 |
| Red pepper flakes or 1 diced jalapeno (optional) | pinch |

### Cilantro-Lime Crema

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Sour cream or Greek yogurt | 1 cup |
| Fresh cilantro, packed | 1 cup |
| Lime | 1 (juice and zest) |
| Garlic clove | 1 |
| Kosher salt | pinch |

### Quick Pickled Red Onions

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Red onion, thinly sliced | 1 |
| Lime juice or white vinegar | 3/4 cup |
| Water | 1/4 cup |
| Kosher salt | 1 tsp |
| Sugar | 1 tsp |

### To Serve

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Corn tortillas | 16 to 24 |
| Shredded cabbage | 2 cups |
| Lime wedges | to taste |
| Sliced jalapenos or hot sauce | to taste |
| Cotija or queso fresco (optional) | to taste |
| Diced pineapple or mango (optional) | to taste |

## Method

1. **Pickle the onions first.** Pack the sliced red onion into a jar. Stir the lime juice or vinegar, water, salt, and sugar together until dissolved, pour over the onion, and press down so it is submerged. Ready in 30 minutes, better after a few hours.
2. **Marinate the pork.** Rub the pork chunks with the salt. In a bowl, whisk the Coca-Cola, lime juice, orange juice, garlic, cumin, oregano, olive oil, and pepper. Toss with the pork. Marinate overnight in the fridge if you can, but even 30 minutes helps.
3. **Pressure cook.** Layer the onion (if using) on the bottom of the Instant Pot, then add the pork and all of the marinade. Seal and cook on high pressure for 50 minutes. Let the pressure release naturally for 15 minutes, then vent the rest.
4. **Shred and reduce.** Move the pork to a bowl and shred it with two forks. Switch the pot to sauté and simmer the cooking liquid for 12 to 15 minutes, until it turns thick and glossy. Toss the pork back in.
5. **Broil for crisp edges.** Spread the pork on a sheet pan and broil 5 to 8 minutes until the edges brown and crisp. Watch it closely, because the sugar in the Coke makes it go from caramelized to burnt quickly. Finish with a squeeze of lime and taste for salt.
6. **Make the crema.** Blend the sour cream or yogurt, cilantro, lime juice and zest, garlic, and salt until smooth. Keep it tart and loose enough to drizzle.
7. **Warm the tortillas.** Char them in a dry skillet or over a gas flame until soft and lightly blistered. Wrap in a towel and keep in a low oven so they stay pliable. Double them up if they are thin.
8. **Build.** Tortilla, pork, cabbage, pickled onions, crema, and whatever else you like.

## Notes

- **Use regular Coke.** Diet will not caramelize or reduce into a glaze.
- **Balance the sweetness.** The Coke version runs sweeter than a plain orange and lime mojo. The pickled onions, extra lime, and tart crema are what keep it in check. A pinch of chili flakes or a diced jalapeno in the marinade helps too.
- **Prefer more savory?** Use half Coke and half orange juice.
- **Do not skip the broiler.** Pressure-cooked pork needs the crispy edges for texture.
- **Pot size.** A 6-quart pot tops out around 4 lb of pork. For a bigger crowd, cook in two batches.
- **Timing for a party.** Start about 2 hours before serving. The pork holds on keep warm for an hour in its juices, then broil right before serving.
- **What to serve with it.** Plain white rice, a cucumber or fennel-citrus salad, and lime wedges. It pairs well next to a creamy coconut curry, since the tart crema and pickled onions cut the richness.
- **Plan on 2 to 3 tacos per person**, so 20 to 24 tortillas for 8 people.
