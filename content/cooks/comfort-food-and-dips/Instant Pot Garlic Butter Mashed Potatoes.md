Skin-on small potatoes pressure cooked with a handful of garlic, then mashed right in the pot with butter and warm milk. It is the easiest way to work through a Costco bag of small potatoes.

## At a Glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Yield | 6 servings |
| Active time | 10 minutes |
| Total time | About 30 minutes |
| Cook method | Instant Pot, High Pressure |

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Small potatoes (baby gold or red), scrubbed | 3 lb |
| Garlic cloves, smashed | 4 |
| Water | 1 cup |
| Kosher salt, for the cooking water | 1 tsp |
| Unsalted butter, cubed | 6 tbsp |
| Whole milk, warmed | 1/2 cup |
| Kosher salt, for finishing | 3/4 tsp, to taste |
| Freshly ground black pepper | 1/2 tsp |
| Fresh chives, finely chopped | 2 tbsp |
| Grated Parmesan (optional) | 1/2 cup |

## Method

1. Scrub the potatoes and leave the skins on. Cut any potato bigger than about 1.5 inches in half so everything cooks evenly. Warm the milk in the microwave for 30 to 45 seconds.
2. Pour the water into the Instant Pot and stir in 1 tsp salt. Add the potatoes and smashed garlic. A trivet or steamer basket works, but dumping them straight into the water is fine too.
3. Lock the lid, set the valve to Sealing, and cook on High Pressure for 7 minutes. Quick release when the timer ends.
4. Carefully drain the potatoes and garlic, then return them to the empty pot. Let them sit uncovered for 1 to 2 minutes so the surface steam escapes. This keeps the mash from turning watery.
5. Add the butter and mash right in the pot with a potato masher. Pour in the milk a splash at a time until the texture is where you want it.
6. Stir in the finishing salt, pepper, chives, and Parmesan if using. Taste and adjust the salt. Hold on the Keep Warm setting with the lid on until dinner.

## Notes

- Mash by hand. Small potatoes turn gluey fast with a mixer or blender.
- For extra richness, swap half the milk for sour cream or cream cheese.
- Other good add-ins: ranch seasoning, roasted garlic, crispy bacon bits, or a little fresh thyme or rosemary.
- If the potatoes are tiny (under an inch), drop the cook time to 5 minutes.
