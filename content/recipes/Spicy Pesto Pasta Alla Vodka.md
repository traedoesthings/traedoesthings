Vodka sauce with basil pesto stirred through and a hit of crushed red pepper. Weeknight-fast, one pan for the sauce plus a pasta pot. Adapted from [Half Baked Harvest's Spicy Pesto Pasta alla Vodka](https://www.halfbakedharvest.com/spicy-pesto-pasta-alla-vodka/).

## At a Glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Yield | 6 servings |
| Prep | 10 minutes |
| Cook | 20 minutes |

## Ingredients

### Sauce

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Extra virgin olive oil | 1/4 cup |
| Shallot, finely chopped | 1 |
| Garlic, finely chopped | 4 cloves |
| Dried oregano | 1 tsp |
| Crushed red pepper flakes | 1 to 2 tsp |
| Tomato paste | 1/2 cup |
| Vodka | 1/4 cup |
| Basil pesto | 3/4 cup |
| Heavy cream (or canned coconut milk) | 1 cup |
| Kosher salt and black pepper | to taste |

### To finish

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Short-cut pasta | 1 lb |
| Salted butter | 3 tbsp |
| Grated Parmesan, plus more for serving | 1/3 cup |
| Fresh basil, roughly chopped | 1/4 cup |

## Method

### Aromatics

Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-low. Add shallot, garlic, and oregano. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the shallot softens and starts to caramelize, about 5 minutes.

### Build the sauce

Stir in the tomato paste and red pepper flakes. Let it cook, stirring, for 4 to 5 minutes; the paste will darken and take on a slightly toasted look.

Pour in the vodka, cook 2 minutes to burn off the sharp alcohol edge.

Stir in the pesto and cream. Season with salt and pepper. Keep warm on the lowest heat while the pasta cooks.

### Cook the pasta

Bring a large pot of well-salted water to a boil. Cook the pasta to al dente per the package. Before draining, scoop out 1.5 cups of pasta water.

### Combine

Add the drained pasta to the sauce with 1/2 cup of the reserved pasta water and the butter. Toss until everything is glossy and coated.

Add the Parmesan and fresh basil, toss again. Thin with more pasta water a splash at a time if the sauce is too thick.

Serve topped with more basil and Parmesan.

## Notes

- **Coconut milk swap** works if you want it dairy-free-ish (still has Parmesan and butter). Use full-fat canned; the sauce is thinner but the flavor holds.
- **Pesto quality matters.** Store-bought is fine but the sauce takes on whatever the pesto brings, so use one you like eating on its own.
- **Reserve the pasta water.** Don't skip this; the starch is what marries the sauce to the noodles.
