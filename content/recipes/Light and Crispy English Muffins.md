Bread-flour English muffins with the classic craggy interior, finished with a skillet sear and a bake to set the crumb. Long process, mostly hands-off. Adapted from [America's Test Kitchen's Light and Crispy English Muffins](https://www.americastestkitchen.com/recipes/17039-light-and-crispy-english-muffins).

## At a Glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Yield | 10 muffins |
| Active | ~1 hour |
| Total | ~12.5 hours (includes 8-16 hr overnight chill) |
| Oven | 400°F |

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Bread flour | 2 1/2 cups (390 g) |
| Instant or rapid-rise yeast | 1 1/2 tsp |
| Table salt | 1 1/4 tsp |
| Water, room temperature | 1 1/3 cups |
| Honey | 1 1/2 tbsp |
| Semolina flour, for dusting | 1/2 cup |

## Method

### Mix

Whisk flour, yeast, and salt in a large bowl. Whisk water and honey together, then pour into the flour mixture. Stir until no dry pockets remain. Cover and rest 20 minutes.

### Stretch and fold

Do two rounds of stretch-and-fold: lift the dough at one edge, stretch upward, fold across the bowl. Rotate 45° and repeat about a dozen times. Cover, rest 20 minutes. Repeat the whole set once more.

### Bulk rise

Flip the dough seam side down, cover, and let rise until doubled, about 1 hour.

### Portion

Lay 10 four-inch parchment squares on a baking sheet. Lightly spray with oil, dust with semolina. Spread more semolina in a 10-inch circle on the counter. Turn the dough out onto it seam side up.

### Shape

Gently press the dough into an even round. Cut lengthwise into 4 strips. Cut the end strips into 2 pieces each; cut the middle strips into 3 pieces each (10 total). Shape each portion by folding the corners into the center, then pinching the sides together to make a rough round. Set seam side down on the parchment squares.

### Overnight chill

Cover and refrigerate 8 to 16 hours. The long cold rest is where the flavor and structure come from.

### Warm up

Pull the sheet from the fridge and let the muffins sit covered at room temperature until puffy and soft, 45 to 60 minutes. About 40 minutes in, put a 12-inch skillet on medium-low to preheat, and heat the oven to 400°F.

### Sear

Bump the skillet to medium. Working in batches of up to 3, invert muffins into the skillet. Don't move them; cook until spotty brown on the bottom, 45 to 60 seconds. Flip and repeat on the second side. Return to the baking sheet.

### Bake

Bake until golden and the largest muffin registers 205°F internal, 8 to 10 minutes.

### Cool

Invert onto a wire rack and cool completely, at least 1 hour. Split with a fork (never a knife) so the nooks stay ragged. Toast to serve.

## Notes

- **Fork-split, don't knife-split.** The cratered interior is the whole point. A knife gives you a smooth cut.
- **Semolina** is what gives the outside that classic gritty crunch. Cornmeal works in a pinch but the texture is different.
- **The overnight chill is not optional.** Attempting to skip it gives you dense muffins with no flavor.
- **Storage:** cool completely, then bag. Room temp 2 days, frozen up to 2 months. Toast from frozen.
