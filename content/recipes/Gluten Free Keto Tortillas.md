Pliable, low-carb tortillas that come together in about 15 minutes. Almond and coconut flour base, held together by xanthan gum and a splash of acid. Adapted from [gnom-gnom's Grain Free Keto Tortillas](https://www.gnom-gnom.com/grain-free-keto-tortillas/).

## At a Glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Yield | 8 tortillas (~5 inches each) |
| Prep | 10 minutes |
| Cook | ~5 minutes total (30-40 seconds per tortilla) |
| Net carbs | ~2 g per tortilla |

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Almond flour | 96 g |
| Coconut flour | 24 g |
| Xanthan gum | 2 tsp |
| Baking powder | 1 tsp |
| Kosher salt | 1/4 tsp |
| Apple cider vinegar | 2 tsp |
| Egg, lightly beaten | 1 |
| Water | 3 tsp |

**Equipment:** food processor (preferred) or bowl + stand/hand mixer; tortilla press or rolling pin; skillet.

## Method

### Dry mix

Add almond flour, coconut flour, xanthan gum, baking powder, and salt to the food processor. Pulse until fully combined.

### Add wet ingredients

With the processor running, pour in the apple cider vinegar. Once distributed, add the egg, then the water. Stop as soon as the dough forms a ball. It will be sticky.

### Knead and rest

Wrap the dough in cling film and knead it through the plastic for a minute or two, like a stress ball. Rest for 10 minutes on the counter, or up to 3 days in the fridge.

### Portion

Divide into 8 equal balls (about 26 g each). Roll each between two sheets of parchment or wax paper to a 5-inch round, or press in a tortilla press (much faster).

### Cook

Heat a skillet over medium. Test the heat with a couple of water droplets: they should skate across the pan, not evaporate on contact. If they vanish immediately, it is too hot.

Lay a tortilla in the pan for just 3 to 6 seconds, then flip immediately with a thin spatula. Cook the second side for 30 to 40 seconds until lightly golden with a few charred spots, then flip once more to finish the first side.

Overcooking kills the pliability. Pull them as soon as they look ready.

### Hold and rewarm

Keep finished tortillas warm wrapped in a kitchen towel. To rewarm later, heat briefly on both sides, under a minute total.

## Notes

- **Best fresh.** Eat the day of if possible; the texture is best right off the pan.
- **Make-ahead:** dough keeps in the fridge for up to 3 days. Freeze cooked tortillas for up to 3 months.
- **Xanthan gum is doing a lot of work here.** It is what makes them fold without cracking. Don't skip it.
- **Very quick first-side flip** is the trick. Long first side equals dry tortilla.
