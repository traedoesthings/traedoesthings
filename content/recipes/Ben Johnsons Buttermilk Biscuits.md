A simple, no-fuss buttermilk biscuit recipe shared by the Ben Johnson Cowboy Museum in Pawhuska, Oklahoma for World Baking Day. Adapted from their [Facebook post](https://www.facebook.com/) with the method rewritten in my own words.

## At a Glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Yield | About 16 to 20 biscuits (2 inch) |
| Oven | 400 degrees F |
| Bake | Until lightly browned |
| Pan | Biscuit pan or similar baking pan |

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| All-purpose flour | 2 cups |
| Baking powder | 2 heaping tsp |
| Sugar | 1 tbsp |
| Baking soda | 1/4 tsp |
| Salt | 1/4 tsp |
| Buttermilk | 1 cup |
| Shortening, melted in the pan | 1/4 cup |

## Method

### Prep

Heat the oven to 400 degrees. Put the shortening in your biscuit pan and let it melt while the oven comes up to temperature.

### Make the dough

Stir the flour, baking powder, sugar, baking soda, and salt together in a bowl. Pour in the buttermilk and mix until the dough comes together.

### Cut and bake

Roll the dough out on a floured board and cut rounds with a biscuit cutter. Dip each biscuit in the melted shortening in the pan, turning it so both sides are coated, then set it in the pan. Bake until the tops are lightly browned.

## Notes

- The recipe gives no bake time, so start checking around the 12 to 15 minute mark and go by color.
- Handle the dough as little as you can once the buttermilk is in. Overworking it makes tougher biscuits.
- Press the cutter straight down without twisting so the edges can rise.
- Cold buttermilk helps keep the biscuits tender.
