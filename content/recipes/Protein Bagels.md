Two-ingredient dough (self-rising flour effect from baking powder plus Greek yogurt) that skips the yeast, the boil, and the overnight proof. Not a traditional bagel texture, but real bread structure and ~10 g of protein each. Adapted from [Little Spice Jar's High Protein Bagels](https://www.littlespicejar.com/protein-bagels/).

## At a Glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Yield | 6 bagels |
| Prep | 15 minutes |
| Cook | 20 to 25 minutes |
| Oven | 350°F |
| Per bagel | ~206 cal, 10 g protein, 35 g carbs, 2 g fat, 2 g fiber |

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| All-purpose or bread flour | 2 1/4 cups |
| Baking powder | 4 tsp |
| Kosher salt | 1 tsp |
| Greek yogurt | 1 1/2 cups |
| Egg white, lightly beaten | 1 |
| Sesame seeds or everything bagel seasoning | for topping |

## Method

### Dry mix

Preheat the oven to 350°F. Line a baking sheet with parchment. In a bowl, whisk the flour, baking powder, and salt together.

### Bring the dough together

Add the Greek yogurt and mix until a shaggy dough forms, then knead in the bowl (or briefly on a floured surface) until it comes together and no dry flour remains. It will be a little tacky; that is fine.

### Shape

Divide the dough into 6 equal pieces. Roll each piece into a rope 7 to 8 inches long, then join the ends together to form a ring. Pinch the seam firmly so it doesn't split in the oven. Space them out on the parchment.

### Rest

Let the shaped bagels sit for 5 to 10 minutes. This gives the baking powder a head start.

### Top and bake

Brush the tops with beaten egg white and scatter with sesame seeds or everything bagel seasoning. Bake 20 to 25 minutes until deep golden brown.

Cool on a wire rack for 10 minutes before slicing.

## Notes

- **Use thick Greek yogurt.** Regular or thin yogurt makes the dough too wet. Full-fat and 2 percent both work; nonfat also fine but slightly drier.
- **Bread flour** gives a chewier crumb than AP; either works.
- **Not a traditional bagel.** No boil, no yeast, so the crust is soft and the crumb is fluffier and more biscuit-like. Great toasted.
- **Storage:** best day-of. Freeze pre-sliced for up to a month; toast straight from frozen.
- **Flavor variations:** stir in 1 tbsp of everything seasoning into the dough, or swap the salt for garlic salt for a savory version. For cinnamon-raisin, add 1 tsp cinnamon and a handful of raisins to the dry mix.
