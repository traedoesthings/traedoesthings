## \$ Everyday

### Guajiros Miami Eatery

A family-run spot serving Cuban and Latin American food in a bright, lively space, with homemade recipes and a warm welcome. My wife and I go here for a breakfast date almost every week. Good for brunch, and easy on the wallet.

**What to order:**

*Appetizers*

- **Queso frito:** Fried cheese with a really good aioli for dipping.
- **Chicken empanadas:** Great as an appetizer, or a full meal if you order both.

*Entrees*

- **The Hands On with pork:** My go-to breakfast burrito, and I eat it about once a week.
- **The Breakfast Bowl:** The same idea as the burrito, served in a bowl. You can also add queso frito.

*Dessert*

- **Tres leches:** Dessert, if you still have room.

*To Drink*

- **Colada:** Classic Cuban-style cafecito, just more of it.
- **Cafe con leche:** A Cuban-style iced latte.

**Address:** 114 10th St NW, Charlottesville ([website](https://www.guajiroscville.com/))

**Parking:** A few small lots are close by, but they tend to fill up on weekends during peak hours. Give yourself extra time.

**Reservations:** Walk-in only, no reservations.

## \$\$ Nice Dinner

### Mockingbird

Southern comfort food with a creative streak, in the heart of Belmont. It is a great spot for outdoor dining when the weather is nice, and the menu makes it hard to order just one thing.

**What to order:**

*Appetizers*

- **Fried green tomato:** Comes with whipped pimento cheese and arugula. The arugula makes it a salad, and I will not be taking questions.
- **Crab rangoon dip:** Lump crab and cream cheese, basically your favorite takeout appetizer in dip form. Zero complaints.
- **Biscuit basket:** Five buttermilk biscuits with spreads. Sharing is optional.

*Entrees*

- **Bison hanger steak:** Local bison with a whiskey demi glace. It's a steak, just with a more interesting backstory than most.
- **Crab stuffed trout:** I had this once as a special and I still think about it. It is not on the regular menu, so ask if it ever shows up again.

*Dessert*

- **Chocolate-bourbon pecan pie:** Served with vanilla whipped cream. Yes, you will be full. Get it anyway.

**Address:** 421 Monticello Rd, Charlottesville ([website](https://www.mockingbird-cville.com/))

**Parking:** There is a lot behind the restaurant, but parking in the Belmont area is always tough. Give yourself extra time.

**Reservations:** Needed on weekends. Book ahead through their website or call (434) 465-6131.

## \$\$\$ Night Out

### Smyrna

Eclectic Mediterranean food with Aegean influences, and one of my favorite places for a nicer night out.

**What to order:**

*Appetizers*

- **Sour cherry gazpacho:** Cold soup that is tangy, a little sweet, and nothing like the gazpacho you are picturing. A great pick for anyone who thinks soup is boring.
- **Meze trio:** All of the dips are good, and I am terrible at choosing, so I get the trio and try them all. Add the crudites for dipping. The two were made for each other.
- **The bread:** It is made in-house, and you will want plenty of it for those dips. Do not think about carbs at the door.

*Entrees*

- **Manti:** Fantastic, and I do not use that word lightly. Tiny dumplings with big flavor.
- **Market fish:** Also great. It changes, so it is a fun little mystery every time. Ask what is on.

*Dessert*

- **Baklava cheesecake:** My go-to. It is two desserts in one, which I consider efficient.
- **Meyer lemon granita:** It is unlike anything I have ever had, and everyone should try it at least once. Trust me on this one.

**Address:** 707 W Main St, Charlottesville ([website](https://www.smyrnacville.com/))

**Parking:** Easy at the train station across the street, but it is paid.

**Reservations:** Almost always needed on the weekends. Book ahead through Resy.

## Good to Know

- Popular spots fill up on weekends and during UVA events, so book ahead when you can.
- Parking downtown is easiest in the city garages or on side streets, and parking rules vary by location.
- Hours and menus change, so double check the restaurant's website or call before you head out.
